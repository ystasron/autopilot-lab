Controls

Click the map to pin a destination and the green car drives there. Change the click menu to flip traffic lights instead. Turn autopilot off to drive with the arrow keys.

How it drives

Sense

Toggle five overlays to see what the green car perceives: LiDAR points, the camera view cone with detection boxes, its lane corridor and the planned path.

Plan

At every intersection the car picks a turn, then follows the lane through it. It signals its turns, slows for corners, stops at red lights, yields to oncoming cars on left turns and waits for people on crosswalks. Other drivers follow the same rules, each with a personality: cautious, normal or hurried.

Act

It steers toward a point ahead on its lane and slows for turns, queues, signals and pedestrians.

Edit sim.js to change the driving logic.

Autopilot Lab

A browser-based self-driving car simulation in a small city.

  • Live perception overlays — LiDAR sweep, camera FOV, labelled detections
  • Real routing: Dijkstra over 48 intersection states, no U-turns
  • Rule-following traffic with cautious, normal and hurried drivers
  • Pedestrians, night mode, and Follow / Free camera modes
